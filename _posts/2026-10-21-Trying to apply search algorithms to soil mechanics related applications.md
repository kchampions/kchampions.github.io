Recently, there have been advances in AI for a game I play often, which may change the way the community has to play the game, with irreversible consequences. While I will keep my personal feelings about that situation out of this post, it did start to get me to think: "Can these methods of learning be applied to testing in soil conditions?". More specifically for piles in offshore wind farms, as it was something I had previously been interested in and have looked into.

The purpose of this project is to understand and see whether the concepts used in the game that I play can be applied in a similar way to the installation of cluster piles for offshore wind foundations in sand.
